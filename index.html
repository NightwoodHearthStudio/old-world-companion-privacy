1. About this policy

This policy describes how the Old World Companion mobile application, published by Nightwood Hearth Studio, handles information.

2. Data collection

The application does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. It does not use advertising, analytics or tracking services and does not request access to location, camera, microphone or contacts.

The support screen contains optional links to Ko-fi and Suppi. They open only after the user selects them. Those services operate under their own privacy policies, and Nightwood Hearth Studio does not control their data processing.

5. Children

The application is a companion utility for a board game and is not directed specifically at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated when the application's functionality or legal requirements change. The current version and effective date will always be available on this page.
